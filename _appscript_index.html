CT Insurer Command Page

One page per insurer. Desk shows open tickets only, split into IT (Ethos platform), Ops (insurer medical operations) and Client (individual-client queries). Milestones, issues and tasks come live from CRM. Every row links to its Zoho record.
Command Page is live from Zoho CRM (Cases, Tasks) + Zoho Desk (open tickets); click Refresh to re-pull. Pipeline is a curated growth list you update via chat. Each milestone links to its Deal, each issue to its Case, each task to its Activity.
